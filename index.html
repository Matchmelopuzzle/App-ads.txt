Privacy Policy for Match Melo Puzzle

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Welcome to Match Melo Puzzle! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you play our game.

About The Game

Match Melo Puzzle is a fun match-3 puzzle game. Swipe to match 3 or more same fruits, blast them, clear levels, earn coins, and unlock new exciting levels.

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information like your name, phone number, email address, or location. The game can be played without creating an account.

Third-Party Services

Our game uses third-party services that may collect anonymous data to show ads and improve performance:

  • Google Play Services - For game services and app performance

These services may collect device information like device ID, advertising ID, and app usage to show relevant ads.

Cookies and Ads

Ads shown in the game are provided by Google AdMob. You may see personalized or non-personalized ads based on your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our game is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

Your game progress like levels and coins are stored only on your device. We do not store your game data on our servers.
